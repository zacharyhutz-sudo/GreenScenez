GreenScenez Landscape Management LLC is a full-service landscape company based in Danielsville, Georgia, serving the Greater Athens area.

We work with homeowners and property managers on everything from weekly lawn care to landscape design and installation. Good maintenance keeps a property looking its best; a well-planned landscape adds beauty and function for years to come.

Whatever the scope, our focus is the same: understand your goals, make practical recommendations and take pride in the finished result.
